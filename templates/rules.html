📜 Server Rules

1. No Deathmatch (DM)

No killing other players without a valid roleplay reason.

2. No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

No using vehicles to kill players without RP reason.

3. No Metagaming

No using OOC information in IC situations.

4. No Powergaming

No forcing actions on other players without giving them a chance.

5. Respect All Players

Treat everyone with respect. No racism, harassment, or hate speech.

6. No Cheating/Hacking

Any use of cheats or third-party software = permanent ban.

7. No Advertising

No advertising other servers or services.

8. Follow Admin Instructions

Admins have the final word. Respect their decisions.

9. No Bug Abuse

Report bugs instead of exploiting them.

10. Roleplay Names Only

Use realistic RP names (e.g. John_Smith).
